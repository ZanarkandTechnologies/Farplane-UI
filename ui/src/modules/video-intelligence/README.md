# Video Intelligence

Video Intelligence is the durable YouTube-analysis branch of Content
Intelligence. The local bridge writes a shared `analyze_youtube` content job to
Convex immediately, turns completed analysis into a cited dossier, and retains
current revision-scoped News contributions alongside narrowly keyed comparable
coverage. A source always yields a dossier, may yield recent coverage, and may
independently yield News.

Content Intelligence is the only public AI Office entrypoint: its Content tab
uses a continuous observed-date feed, while News exposes this module's cited
reporting. Related coverage reads persisted current-revision edges and appears
only when another recent creator covers the same development or active
discussion. Existing Resource Bank YouTube assets appear as legacy dossiers; a
later Video Intelligence analysis adds structured claims, stories, concepts,
and perspectives.

## Workflow

1. Browse Content by observed date; reaching the end appends older populated
   days without manual date controls. News retains only current, editorially
   eligible reporting with a direct original-source link, while reviewed
   comparison edges supply dossier-scoped Related coverage.
2. Open a video dossier without leaving the panel.
3. Open a linked story to inspect reporting chronology, perspectives,
   shared/source-specific claims, related events, and timestamp evidence.
4. Use Back to return to the same tab, query/filter state, loaded date groups,
   and scroll context.

The information-flow section is read-only: source edges mean `contributes` and
story edges mean conservatively `related`. It never claims citation or
causality. Publisher scoring, tag rename/merge governance, editable graphs, and
multi-user editorial permissions remain deferred.
